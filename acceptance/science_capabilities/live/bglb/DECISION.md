# Closing decision

The published BglB pNPG panel supports a limited descriptive rank association among scored, quantified held-out variants. The S1 row data define the reference comparison and denominators.

The joint retrospective found weak positive ordering by negative S3 conservation (Spearman ρ = 0.259626). Of 41 variants held out by residue position, 30 variants had quantified S1 efficiencies and 14 variants also had matching S3 scores. Every eligible row was an alanine substitution at a distinct site. Upper bounds, undetectable soluble yield and missing S3 entries account for the exclusions. The unchanged S1 WT had median absolute log10 error 0.388205 log10 on the same pairs. Its constant prediction makes rank correlation undefined. S1 has fewer mutants with quantifiable soluble yield, more efficiencies below detection and a higher WT point estimate than the article text. Use the traceable S1 rows for the historical comparison and preserve the discrepancy. Rerunning the fixed method on these same tables would add no information about the discrepancy or generalization.

## Limitations

- The source records do not explain why the article text and S1 report different counts and WT central estimates.
- Selective score coverage and unquantified outcomes leave performance for excluded variants unknown. The study provides no independent test of generalization.
- The registered scope covered the S1 acquisition alone, so the joint S1 and S3 result cannot formally qualify as prospective confirmation.
