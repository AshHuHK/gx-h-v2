In the production-profile control, the v2.1 and P1 native startup p95s were **1,670 and 1,837 ms** across eight cold starts per release. Both p95s are sample maxima. For P1, the observation at p95 spent 690 ms in official initialization, 411 ms in account retrieval and 704 ms in model catalog retrieval. Preparing the P1 package and worker tools inside the timed dispatch took at most 1.24 ms across this control. ## Paid cold intervals

All 13 paid arrivals completed. Across the 18 Runs with candidate process-birth measurements in waves 4 through 12, the baseline’s entire dispatch-to-first-GX-writer interval reached 2,435 ms p95. The candidate spent at least 5,023 ms p95 **after official child birth** through that same writer. With 18 samples each, these p95s are maxima. Every candidate prebirth interval was at most 123.1 ms. The conservative postbirth bound exceeded the matching baseline Run’s entire cold interval in 16 of 18 cases, with a median paired surplus of 753 ms. Source inspection places package and worker-tool assembly before the official child is created. The subsequent official metadata requests and process scheduling occur before the first GX writer. The paid logs did not time those individual requests.
