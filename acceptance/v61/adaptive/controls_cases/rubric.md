# V6.1 matched controls

The primary endpoint is the fraction of the five prespecified native trials per case that (1) pass the pinned product source, consequence, spending, scoped-access and cold-recovery checks, (2) cite all injected measurements, and (3) reach the structured decision below. The actual tool submission, claim text, rationale and Job receipts accompany each score. The three public conditions originate in the V6 Agent Kit. Two additional cases were isolated from runtime/prompt development and from the Lead's filesystem. Synthetic measurements test decisions about already deposited coordinates.

| Case | Primary scientific endpoint |
|---|---|
| p17 | The adopted limited-support claim covers precisely the two eligible comparisons. |
| p42 | A mixed claim covers the two eligible pairs, or separately adopted limited-support and contrary claims distinguish the stable and adverse pair. |
| p63 | The metadata event leaves science, jobs and other existing commitments in place. |
| h81 | Both observations miss a coverage requirement. The adopted claim records insufficient evidence; neither a small change nor a large change reaches a scientific conclusion about robustness. |
| h92 | Coverage and both error limits are inclusive. A single eligible pair supports a limited claim about precisely that pair. |

Every native window is checked with the exact original and product `verify_run` implementations and the registered suite. Grouped public triplets additionally run both unchanged full verifiers with the original three-case roster. A frozen V6 CLI has no whole-suite mode for the new five-case roster. Keep its original verdict alongside the product verdict. A decision that chooses an informative orthogonal recheck without writing the target claim can be scientifically reasonable while missing this strictly defined primary endpoint. Report such decisions in an independent qualitative review; their primary score stays unchanged.

Commit to five randomized blocks. Each block contains one of each condition. All slots use separate states and official native sessions, identical model and tools, one paid call per slot, and a matched prestate. The intervention is delivered once. No replacement or rescoring with altered instructions. Report passes, attempted, not-run, and the planned denominator of five per case and ten for held-outs. Treat absent decisions, incompatible source records and timeouts as failures for attempted trials. An unlaunched trial has no observed scientific result. Do not pool offline or historical observations with live trials.
