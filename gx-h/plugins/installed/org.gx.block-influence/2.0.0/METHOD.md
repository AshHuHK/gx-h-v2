# Deposited three-chain segment comparison, version 2

Choose a registered reference interval (21–40 or 101–120), a mask (common α-carbons or common complete N/Cα/C/O), and the primary quantity (unsigned fit rotation in degrees or fit RMSD in Å). Record your predictions and the consequences for reporting before material release. The operator supplies the original mmCIF and frozen polymer-position map. Chain labels identify copies within this deposition. A reference residue without a target mapping is listed as missing.

Submit one CPU Job with capability `deposited-block-influence@2`, inputs `[structure, mapping]` and parameters `interval` (`21-40` or `101-120`), `mask` (`common_ca` or `common_backbone`), and `endpoint` (`angle_deg` or `rmsd_A`). The worker fits each pair on the common observed positions of the three specified label chains and then refits after omitting the mapped positions. Positive-occupancy unambiguous matching atoms and identical residue identities are required. Eligibility requires at least 60 matched positions and 60% coverage of their observed union, at least ten actually omitted and at least 60 positions and 60% retained.

Read the published `segment.json` with the Goal artifact reader. Its four rows give the actual label pairs and all pre/post values and absolute changes. The representative change is the absolute difference between the two medians. Compare unrounded differences with 0.5° or 0.15 Å. Exact equality is within the limit; differences within 0.02° or 0.002 Å of the limit are marked as borderline. A pair over the limit with its median within the limit indicates a concealed exception for this interval. Unmeasurable rows have null values and reasons. Only the chosen endpoint and mask are reported. Published table reads reuse the same CPU result. `pair12`, `pair13`, and `pair23` refer to the ordered chain labels in the original map; they do not assert that a chain matches a copy in another deposition.
