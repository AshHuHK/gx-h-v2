Use gx_read_source on an assigned text, Markdown or JATS XML artifact. List its segment identifiers, then read the original passage and its surroundings. Record the source reference, version or SHA, stable locator and a short verbatim quotation. Identify what was measured, the sample or material, conditions and the reported result. State separately any proposed mechanism or interpretation. A citation in a secondary document identifies a lead for reading the original. Use the registered text to support only what it actually says.

For evidence you extract, call gx_extract_evidence with the returned segment_id and your own receipt_id, a quote from that segment, the claim, direction, applicability and confidence_basis. Include supporting, opposing or unresolved evidence as warranted. In a separately delegated review, read the original yourself, compare the published extract with it and submit gx_review_evidence with your own read receipt. Carry previous adverse or unresolved judgments into the report. A public engineering fixture retains its fixture status.

Explain which competing interpretation is still viable, whether two citations share an underlying sample or analysis, and which missing observation would distinguish the alternatives. Use report findings to cite the authorized artifacts behind the reading and refer to the accepted evidence or review IDs in the text. Preserve disagreement and report any inaccessible or missing source in gaps. Keep the distinction between a reported observation, the authors' inference and your proposed mechanism.
